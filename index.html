Voice Calculator Guide & Architecture

🎙️ Voice Commands Guide

Click the microphone button (or press Spacebar) and speak naturally into your microphone:

  • Addition: "What is 25 plus 40?", "Add 15 and 35"
  • Subtraction: "100 minus 35", "Subtract 15 from 50"
  • Multiplication: "Multiply 12 by 8", "12 times 8"
  • Division: "Divide 100 by 4", "100 divided by 4"
  • Percentage: "What is 15 percent of 200?", "20 percent of 80"
  • Square Root: "Square root of 144", "What is root 64"
  • Power: "2 to the power of 8", "5 squared", "3 cubed"
  • Trigonometry: "Sine of 30", "Cosine of 60", "Tangent of 45"
  • Voice Controls: "Clear", "Reset", "Delete", "Equals", "Calculate"

⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts

  • 0-9, +, -, *, /: Standard input
  • Enter or =: Calculate result
  • Backspace: Delete last digit
  • Escape: Clear all (AC)
  • Spacebar: Toggle microphone listening

  • Automated Testing: 33+ comprehensive pytest test cases validating mathematical precision, edge cases, and exception handling without unsafe eval().
